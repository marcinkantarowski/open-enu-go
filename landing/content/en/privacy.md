## Who we are

We are the controller of personal data processed through this website and the application. Our
contact details are on the [contact page](/contact).

## What we collect

- **Account data** - your name, email address and password hash, to let you sign in.
- **Workspace data** - the content you and your team store, processed on your organisation's behalf.
- **Technical data** - IP address and request logs, kept for a limited time for security.

## Why we process it

To provide the service you signed up for (contract), to keep it secure (legitimate interest)
and to meet legal obligations such as accounting.

## How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists, and afterwards only as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export or erase your personal data, object to processing and complain
to your supervisory authority. Export and erasure are built into the product.
