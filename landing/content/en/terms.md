We provide access to the application under the plan you choose. We may improve and change it
over time, and will give notice before removing anything you rely on.

You are responsible for the activity in your workspace and for keeping your credentials and API
keys secret. Tell us straight away if you suspect they have been compromised.

## Your data

You own the content you store. We process it only to provide the service, as described in the
[privacy policy](/privacy).

## Acceptable use

Do not use the service to break the law, to attack it or other users, or to try to reach data
that belongs to another workspace.

## Payment and cancellation

Paid plans are billed in advance. You can cancel at any time; the plan stays active until the
end of the paid period.

## Liability

The service is provided with reasonable care. Our liability is limited to the fees you paid in
the twelve months before the claim, except where the law does not allow such a limit.
